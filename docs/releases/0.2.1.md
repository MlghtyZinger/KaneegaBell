New in Settings → General → Storage and reset:

- Clear downloaded data: deletes the covers, backgrounds, logos, game info and achievement data KB downloaded, then fetches them again in the background. Your games, play time, favorites, settings and keys stay, and so do the covers and matches you picked yourself.
- Reset KaneegaBell: back to a fresh start (optionally also removing your saved keys). A copy of the old library is kept as library.before-reset.db in case you change your mind.

Neither touches your game files, emulators or saves.
